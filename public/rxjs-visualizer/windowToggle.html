windowToggle

Opens a window at every value of an openings observable and closes it when the observable returned for that opening first emits; windows may overlap.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, the closings as timers of the same length, each window flattened as index:value

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const openings = merge(at(2, 'x'), at(14, 'y'), at(23, 'z'), end(27));
const closings = [timer(15), timer(4), timer(14)];
let n = 0;
merge(at(4, 'b'), at(8, 'c'), at(12, 'd'), at(16, 'e'), at(20, 'f'), at(24, 'g'), at(28, 'h'), end(35))
  .pipe(windowToggle(openings, () => closings[n++]), mergeMap((w, i) => w.pipe(map((v) => i + ':' + v))))

3 essential criteria

openings
e2, a cold: x at 2, y at 14, z at 23 each open a window and subscribe the next closing
closings
three hot observables; the first value of each (its s) closes its window: at 17, 18, and never before the source ends
overlap
the first window runs 2 to 17 and the second 14 to 18, so e at 16 falls into both

Resulting sink

0:b at 4, 0:c at 8, 0:d at 12, 0:e at 16, 1:e at 16, 2:g at 24, 2:h at 28, complete at 35

Workflow

  1. x at 2 opens window 0; b, c, d fall into it. y at 14 opens window 1 while window 0 is still open.
  2. e at 16 goes into both; window 0 closes at 17 and window 1 at 18 when their closings emit s.
  3. z at 23 opens window 2, which takes g and h; the source completes at 35 and closes it, the third closing never having fired.

In the spec

Each closing lane has a subscription bar from its opening to its s; the 3, 4 and 5 before the bars drop nothing. Below the output lane, window rows 1 and 2 overlap in the column of e at 16, which appears in both.
