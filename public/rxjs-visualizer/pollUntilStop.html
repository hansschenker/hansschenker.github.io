poll until stop

Polling: every tick starts a request, and a stop signal ends the whole pipeline, request in flight included.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const request = merge(at(2, 'r'), end(3));
const stop = at(14, 's');
merge(at(0, 't'), at(4, 't'), at(8, 't'), at(12, 't'), at(16, 't'), end(20))
  .pipe(switchMap(() => request), takeUntil(stop))

3 essential criteria

the ticks
every 4 frames from 0; each one subscribes the request
the request
a cold that answers r after 2 frames and completes
the stop
a notifier at 14: takeUntil completes the output there and unsubscribes everything above it

Resulting sink

r at 2, r at 6, r at 10, complete at 14

Workflow

  1. Ticks at 0, 4 and 8 each start a request that answers at 2, 6 and 10.
  2. The tick at 12 starts a request due at 14; the stop arrives at 14 too and is delivered first, so the output completes and the request is cut without answering.
  3. The ticks at 16 and the completion at 20 happen on a source nobody listens to anymore.

In the spec

The stop lane sits right above the takeUntil row and drops its s onto it at 14. Inner 4 is a bar from 12 to 14 with no marble, and the tick lane's subscription bar ends at 14 while its marbles go on.
