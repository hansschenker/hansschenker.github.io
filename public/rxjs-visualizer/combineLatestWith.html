combineLatestWith

Emits the latest value of every input whenever any input emits, once each input has emitted at least once.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const e2 = merge(at(2, 1), at(5, 2), at(7, 3), at(9, 4), end(13));
merge(at(1, 'a'), at(4, 'b'), at(10, 'c'), at(12, 'd'), at(14, 'e'), end(16)).pipe(
  combineLatestWith(e2),
  map(([letter, n]) => letter + n),
)

3 essential criteria

inputs
letters at 1, 4, 10, 12, 14 and numbers at 2, 5, 7, 9
first emission
only once every input has a value: a1 at 2, not at 1
then
every value of either input emits the latest pair; completion when both have completed

Resulting sink

a1 at 2, b1 at 4, b2 at 5, b3 at 7, b4 at 9, c4 at 10, d4 at 12, e4 at 14, complete at 16

Workflow

  1. a at 1 is stored, but e2 has nothing yet: no output. 1 at 2 completes the pair: a1.
  2. b at 4 gives b1; 2, 3, 4 at 5, 7, 9 give b2, b3, b4: either side triggers.
  3. e2 completes at 13 and its 4 stays the latest: c, d, e give c4, d4, e4. The output completes at 16 with e1.

In the spec

Nothing at frame 1; A appears at 2 when e2 emits; from then on every value of either lane drops onto the operator row and produces a pair, B at 4 and C at 5 back to back.
