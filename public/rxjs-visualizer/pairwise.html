pairwise

Emits every value together with the one before it, as a pair.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
merge(at(2, 'a'), at(5, 'b'), at(7, 'c'), at(12, 'd'), at(15, 'e'), end(19))
  .pipe(pairwise())

3 essential criteria

the first value
a at 2 has no predecessor and emits nothing
every later value
emits [previous, current] on its own frame
n values
give n - 1 pairs: five values, four pairs

Resulting sink

[a, b] at 5, [b, c] at 7, [c, d] at 12, [d, e] at 15, complete at 19

Workflow

  1. a at 2 is remembered, nothing is emitted.
  2. b at 5 emits [a, b]; c at 7 emits [b, c].
  3. d at 12 emits [c, d], e at 15 emits [d, e]; the completion at 19 passes.

In the spec

a at 2 emits nothing; every value from b on emits a pill with itself and the value before it, on its own frame.
