Scheduler · start

refCount

The number of subscribers decides the connection: the first one connects the source, the last to leave disconnects it, and a later one connects it again from the start.

Sample: the drawn spec as code: two subscribers that leave, a third that comes after

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const shared = merge(at(1, 1), at(3, 2), at(5, 3), at(10, 4), end(12)).pipe(publish(), refCount());
let s1, s2;
timer(3).subscribe(() => { s1 = shared.subscribe((v) => log('s1', v)); });
timer(7).subscribe(() => { s2 = shared.subscribe((v) => log('s2', v)); });
timer(10).subscribe(() => s1.unsubscribe());
timer(12).subscribe(() => s2.unsubscribe());
timer(15).subscribe(() => shared.subscribe((v) => log('s3', v)));

4 essential criteria

the count
1 at 3, 2 at 7, 1 at 10, 0 at 12, 1 at 15, 0 at 27: one up for every subscriber joining, one down for every one leaving
connect
when the count goes from 0 to 1, at 3 and again at 15; each time the cold starts from its beginning
disconnect
when it falls back to 0: at 12 the source is unsubscribed, so the 4 it would emit at 13 reaches no one
not an rxjs test
the two subscribers of share's `should disconnect when last subscriber unsubscribes`, plus a third that joins after the count fell to 0

Resulting sink

s1 1 at 4, s1 2 at 6, s1 3 at 8, s2 3 at 8, s3 1 at 16, s3 2 at 18, s3 3 at 20, s3 4 at 25

Workflow

  1. Nothing happens before 3: publish() made the source connectable and no one has subscribed. s1 subscribes at 3, the count is 1, refCount connects: the cold is subscribed and emits 1 at 4 and 2 at 6.
  2. s2 joins at 7 (count 2) and shares the running connection: 3 at 8 reaches both. s1 leaves at 10 (count 1), s2 at 12 (count 0): the connection is unsubscribed, and the 4 the cold would have emitted at 13 never happens.
  3. s3 joins at 15 (count 1 again): refCount connects a second time and the cold starts over, so s3 gets 1, 2, 3, 4 at 16, 18, 20, 25 and the completion at 27, which takes the count to 0.

In the spec

Watch the refCount row: a count pill at every join and leave, and a connection bar exactly while the count is above 0. Each bar is one connection row of the source above it; the second connection starts the cold from its first marble again, and the 4 at 13 on connection 1 sits right of its bar, produced for no one.
