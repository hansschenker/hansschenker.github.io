mergeAll

Subscribes to every inner observable the source emits, all at once, and merges their values.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const x = merge(at(2, 'a'), at(6, 'b'), at(9, 'c'), at(13, 'd'), end(16));
const y = merge(at(4, 'e'), at(8, 'f'), at(11, 'g'), end(15));
merge(at(2, x), at(9, y), end(17))
  .pipe(mergeAll())

3 essential criteria

source values
observables: x at 2 and y at 9; mergeAll is mergeMap with an identity project
concurrency
unlimited: each inner is subscribed the frame it arrives, and its frames count from there
completion
when the source, at 17, and every inner have completed: y at 24

Resulting sink

a at 4, b at 8, c at 11, e at 13, d at 15, f at 17, g at 20, complete at 24

Workflow

  1. x arrives at 2 and is subscribed: a, b, c, d at 4, 8, 11, 15, complete at 18.
  2. y arrives at 9 and is subscribed while x still runs: e, f, g at 13, 17, 20, complete at 24.
  3. the output interleaves both by time, e at 13 between c and d; the source completes at 17, the output at 24 with y.

In the spec

The source lane carries x and y, two cold observables; each gets an inner row with a subscription bar from the frame it arrived, and the output lane interleaves a, b, c, e, d, f, g.
