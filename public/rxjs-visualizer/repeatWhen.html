repeatWhen

Resubscribes the source after it completes, each time an observable built from its completions emits; completes when that notifier completes.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, the hot notifier as a Subject fed by timers

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const notifier = new Subject();
timer(13).subscribe(() => notifier.next('r'));
timer(26).subscribe(() => notifier.next('r'));
timer(28).subscribe(() => notifier.complete());
merge(at(1, 1), at(4, 2), end(7))
  .pipe(repeatWhen(() => notifier))
  .subscribe({ next: log, complete: () => log('complete') });

3 essential criteria

the notifier
hot, subscribed when the source first completes at 7; each r at 13 and 26 subscribes the source again
between
after a completion the output waits silently for the next r
the notifier completing
at 28, during the third run: the run goes on to its end and the output completes with it at 33

Resulting sink

1 at 1, 2 at 4, 1 at 14, 2 at 17, 1 at 27, 2 at 30, complete at 33

Workflow

  1. The cold runs from 0: 1 at 1, 2 at 4, completes at 7; the notifier is subscribed there.
  2. r at 13 starts attempt 2 (1 at 14, 2 at 17, complete at 20); r at 26 starts attempt 3.
  3. The notifier completes at 28; attempt 3 finishes at 33 and the output completes.

In the spec

Three attempt rows of the cold, the second and third starting in the columns where the notifier lane has its r values, which drop onto the operator row; the notifier’s bar starts at 7, not at 0.
