groupBy

Routes each value into a group chosen by its key, emitting every group as an observable of its own the first time its key appears.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, with each group flattened as key:value

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
merge(at(2, 1), at(6, 2), at(10, 3), at(14, 4), at(18, 5), end(22))
  .pipe(groupBy(n => n % 2), mergeMap(group => group.pipe(map(n => group.key + ':' + n))))

4 essential criteria

the key
n % 2: 1 for odd values, 0 for even ones
a new key
emits a new group observable on the output, at the frame of its first value
a known key
pushes the value into the existing group; the output lane sees nothing
on completion
every group completes with the source

Resulting sink

1:1 at 2, 0:2 at 6, 1:3 at 10, 0:4 at 14, 1:5 at 18, complete at 22

Workflow

  1. 1 at 2 has a new key: group 1 opens on the output lane and receives 1.
  2. 2 at 6 has a new key: group 0 opens and receives 2.
  3. 3, 4 and 5 go into the existing groups at 10, 14 and 18; the output lane stays quiet. Both groups complete with the source at 22.

In the spec

The output lane has two marbles, the keys 1 and 0, and under it two rows, one per group: each hangs from the frame it opened and carries its own values, the rings being the cold the test expects.
