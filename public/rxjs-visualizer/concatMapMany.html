Scheduler · start

concatMap

concatMap with values arriving faster than the inners finish: every value waits its turn and none is lost.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const inner = merge(at(2, 'foo'), at(4, 'bar'), at(6, 'baz'), at(8, 'qux'), end(10));
merge(at(1, 'a'), at(5, 'b'), at(9, 'c'), at(13, 'd'), end(17))
  .pipe(concatMap(() => inner))

3 essential criteria

outer
a, b, c, d, one every 4 frames
inner
four values over 10 frames, longer than the gap between outer values
queue
b, c and d each wait until the inner before them completes; none is lost

Resulting sink

foo at 3, bar at 5, baz at 7, qux at 9, foo at 13, bar at 15, baz at 17, qux at 19, foo at 23, bar at 25, baz at 27, qux at 29, foo at 33, bar at 35, baz at 37, qux at 39, complete at 41

Workflow

  1. a at 1 starts an inner that runs to 11: foo, bar, baz, qux at 3, 5, 7 and 9.
  2. b at 5 arrives while it runs and waits until 11; c at 9 and d at 13 queue up behind it, each inner starting when the one before completes, at 11, 21 and 31.
  3. the source completes at 17, long before the queue is done; the output completes at 41 with the last inner.

In the spec

Every inner row after the first hangs from its value with a dotted queued span reaching the frame the previous inner completed; the four subscription bars line up end to end, and the source lane is finished while three inners are still to run.
