repeat

On completion, resubscribes to the source, up to the given number of times in total.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
merge(at(2, 'a'), at(5, 'b'), end(8))
  .pipe(repeat(3))

3 essential criteria

count
3 subscriptions in total, at 0, 8 and 16
what is repeated
the whole source, subscribed again on the frame it completes
errors
are not repeated; they pass through

Resulting sink

a at 2, b at 5, a at 10, b at 13, a at 18, b at 21, complete at 24

Workflow

  1. a at 2 and b at 5 pass and the source completes at 8; repeat subscribes again at 8.
  2. the second run emits a at 10 and b at 13 and completes at 16; the third starts at 16.
  3. the third run emits a at 18 and b at 21; its completion at 24 is the last, so the output completes.

In the spec

Three attempt rows, each a full subscription of the cold ending in its completion, each hanging from the end of the one before; the output completes with the third, at 24.
