timeoutWith

Like timeout, but when the deadline passes it switches to another observable instead of erroring.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const switchTo = merge(at(0, 'x'), at(2, 'y'), at(4, 'z'), end(6));
merge(at(7, 'a'), at(10, 'b'), end(13))
  .pipe(timeoutWith(5, switchTo))

3 essential criteria

the deadline
5 frames from the subscription, the spec's t; the source's first value would be due at 7
at the deadline
the source is unsubscribed at 5 and switchTo subscribed, its cold marbles counting from there
the rest
a and b of the source are never seen; the output is switchTo to its completion at 11

Resulting sink

x at 5, y at 7, z at 9, complete at 11

Workflow

  1. The subscription at 0 starts a 5-frame deadline; nothing arrives.
  2. At 5 the deadline passes: the source is dropped and switchTo subscribed, emitting x at once.
  3. y at 7 and z at 9 follow; switchTo completes at 11 and the output with it.

In the spec

The source bar ends at 5, where the deadline window on the operator row ends and the switchTo bar begins; a and b sit on the source lane after its bar, unseen. The measure of t at the top of the row is exactly the deadline.
