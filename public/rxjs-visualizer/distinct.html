distinct

Drops a value that was seen before, anywhere in the stream.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
merge(at(2, 'a'), at(5, 'a'), at(8, 'a'), at(11, 'b'), at(14, 'b'), at(17, 'a'), end(20))
  .pipe(distinct())

3 essential criteria

memory
a Set of every value seen so far
a value in the Set
is dropped: the a at 17, although a b came between
a new value
passes and is added: a at 2, b at 11

Resulting sink

a at 2, b at 11, complete at 20

Workflow

  1. a at 2 is new and passes; the a at 5 and the a at 8 are dropped.
  2. b at 11 is new and passes; the b at 14 is dropped.
  3. the a at 17 was seen at 2 and is dropped, unlike with distinctUntilChanged; the completion at 20 passes.

In the spec

Only the first a at 2 and the first b at 11 reach the output; the a at 17 has no marble, because a was seen before.
