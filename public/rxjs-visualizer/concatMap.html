concatMap

Maps every value to an inner observable and runs the inners one after another, in order.

Sample: the drawn spec as code, one frame per millisecond

// at(ms, v): v at ms; end(ms): complete at ms; 1 frame = 1 ms
const at = (ms, v) => timer(ms).pipe(map(() => v));
const end = (ms) => timer(ms).pipe(ignoreElements());
const inner = merge(at(0, 10), at(2, 10), at(4, 10), end(5));
merge(at(2, 1), at(8, 3), at(11, 5), end(19))
  .pipe(concatMap((value) => inner.pipe(map((i) => i * value))))

3 essential criteria

project
the same inner and scaling as mergeMap
concurrency
one: a value arriving while an inner runs waits in a queue
order
inner after inner, in the order the values arrived; nothing overlaps

Resulting sink

10 at 2, 10 at 4, 10 at 6, 30 at 8, 30 at 10, 30 at 12, 50 at 13, 50 at 15, 50 at 17, complete at 19

Workflow

  1. 1 at 2 starts an inner that completes at 7: 10 at 2, 4 and 6.
  2. 3 at 8 finds nothing running and starts at once: 30 at 8, 10 and 12, complete at 13. 5 at 11 arrives while that runs and waits.
  3. at 13 the queue moves on: 5's inner emits 50 at 13, 15 and 17. The output completes at 19 with the source.

In the spec

The inner row of 5 shows a dotted queued span from 11, when 5 arrived, to 13, when 3's inner completed and the subscription bar starts.
